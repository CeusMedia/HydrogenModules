Privacy policy

Data storage

All user-related data is stored exclusively for providing the service.
A session cookie is stored for the site to work. There are no other cookies.

Incoming e-mails to a group are stored in order to:

  • show the content to the manager of the group for review (required by law)
  • comply with the obligation to provide evidence in case of violations of applicable law

Data transmission

Data between the browser and this service is transmitted encrypted. The internal communication with the mail server is encrypted as well. Interception of data by third parties via man-in-the-middle attacks is therefore not possible.

Data disclosure

No data is passed on to third parties.
This site does not use any external tracking services.
