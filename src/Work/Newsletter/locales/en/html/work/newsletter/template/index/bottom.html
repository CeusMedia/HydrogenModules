Explanation

Templates are the foundation of a campaign.
While each campaign has its own title and content, the template provides a consistent frame for the campaigns.

A template consists of skeletons for text and HTML mails as well as style definitions.
Existing templates can be exported and new templates can be imported.

For both HTML and text mails, the template can provide the following building blocks:

  • Design and content of the mail header
  • Arrangement of the campaign contents (title and content)
  • Imprint and other legally required contents
  • Design and content of the mail footer
