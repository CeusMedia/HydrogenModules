 Tasks

Tasks refer to a day without a specific time. They are meant to be either started or finished on that day. They are shown in the list view until they have been completed.

 Events

Events refer to a day and a time of day.
The list view also shows past events until they have been closed.


Tasks can be converted to events and vice versa.

Tip: Synchronization

Tasks and events can be synchronized with calendar applications, such as Apple iCal, Microsoft Outlook or Mozilla Thunderbird.
